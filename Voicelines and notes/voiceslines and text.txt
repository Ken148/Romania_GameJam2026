Floor 1:
--------------------journal--------------------------

May 3

Mihai Petrescu
Marine Systems Technician
M/V Horizon Dawn

Fifth month aboard the Horizon Dawn.

When I first accepted this contract, I thought I would only be away from home for a few months. Fix machines, inspect equipment, collect my pay, and return to Constanța.

The ocean always sounds easier from the shore.

May 11

Working on cargo ships is different. Every day there is something new breaking. Pumps fail, generators need repairs, pipes leak. The ship is like a living thing. If you ignore one small problem, eventually it becomes a much bigger one.

The crew has been good to me. Long hours, bad coffee, and too many arguments about football, but they are good people.

May 19

The last few weeks have been strange.

The captain ordered extra inspections after several failures in the lower decks. Some systems are older than they should be, and replacement parts are becoming harder to find.

May 26

I have been spending most of my time below deck repairing electrical systems and checking the emergency equipment.

Something about this voyage feels wrong.

Maybe it is just exhaustion.

May 29

Tomorrow we continue through rough waters. I need to finish checking the lower compartments before my next shift.

Hopefully after this trip I can finally go home.
